# 🧭 Tumkur Tourism AI Agent

> **Discover Tumakuru. Plan smarter. Explore more.**

Tumkur Tourism AI Agent is an **AI-powered tourism assistant** built to help visitors discover, explore, and plan trips across **Tumakuru (Tumkur), Karnataka**.

Instead of providing a static list of tourist destinations, the platform acts as a conversational travel companion that helps users find places based on their interests, available time, budget, and travel preferences.

---

## ✨ What It Does

The AI agent helps users:

* 🏛️ Discover historical and cultural attractions
* 🌄 Find nature and sightseeing destinations
* 🛕 Explore temples and heritage locations
* 🍽️ Discover local food and experiences
* 📍 Find places based on location
* 🗺️ Generate personalized travel itineraries
* ⏱️ Plan trips based on available time
* 💰 Consider budget and travel preferences
* 💬 Ask questions about destinations conversationally
* 📌 Discover nearby attractions and experiences

---

## 🎯 Problem

People visiting Tumakuru often have to search across multiple websites, maps, blogs, and social platforms to decide:

* Where should I go?
* What can I cover in one day?
* Which places are near each other?
* What should I visit based on my interests?
* Where can I eat nearby?
* What is the best route for my trip?

Tumkur Tourism AI Agent brings these interactions into **one AI-powered experience**.

---

## 💡 Solution

The platform combines **AI-powered recommendations, tourism information, location awareness, and itinerary planning** into a single conversational interface.

A user can simply ask:

> "I have one day in Tumakuru. I like nature and historical places. What should I visit?"

The AI can use the user's preferences to generate a structured travel plan with relevant destinations and activities.

---

## 🚀 Core Features

### 🤖 AI Tourism Assistant

A conversational interface that allows users to ask natural-language questions about Tumakuru tourism.

### 🗺️ Smart Itinerary Planning

Generate travel plans based on:

* Available time
* Interests
* Budget
* Number of travelers
* Preferred activities
* Starting location

### 📍 Location-Based Discovery

Help users discover attractions and experiences based on their location and selected destinations.

### 🏛️ Cultural & Heritage Discovery

Explore temples, historical landmarks, cultural sites, and other heritage destinations around Tumakuru.

### 🌄 Nature & Adventure

Discover hills, viewpoints, waterfalls, parks, trekking locations, and other outdoor experiences.

### 🍽️ Local Experiences

Help users discover local food, restaurants, and regional experiences.

### 💬 Conversational Recommendations

Users can refine recommendations naturally:

> "Give me something closer."

> "Make it cheaper."

> "Remove temples."

> "Add more nature spots."

> "Can I complete this in one day?"
